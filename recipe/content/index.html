Filet Mignon with Red Wine Sauce

My recipe page will focus on an elevated steak dinner with a red wine and balsamic reduction. I want the final design to feel homey and sophisticated, while still making the recipe easy to follow.

Recipe Content

This recipe combines pan-seared filet mignon with a rich red wine and balsamic sauce. The steak is first seared for a browned exterior, finished in the oven, and served with a reduced sauce that adds sweetness and acidity.

Serves: 2 people

Planning Time: About 1 hour

Oven Temperature: 415°F

Source: Kitchen Swagger by Shawn Williams

The original recipe lists the total time as about 25 minutes, but it also says to let the steaks sit out for 30 minutes before cooking. Because of this, I would plan for closer to one hour from start to finish.

Ingredients

For the Steak

  • 2 filet mignon steaks, about 8–10 oz each
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable or canola oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 sprig fresh rosemary
  • 1 whole garlic clove
  • Optional chopped parsley or rosemary for garnish

For the Sauce

  • 1/2 cup dry red wine
  • 1/2 cup balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 tablespoon packed brown sugar
  • Pinch of salt and black pepper

Instructions

  1. Take the steaks out of the refrigerator about 30 minutes before cooking. Pat them dry and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Preheat the oven to 415°F.
  3. Heat a light coating of oil in an oven-safe skillet over high heat. Sear the steaks for about 2 minutes on each side.
  4. Add the rosemary and garlic, then transfer the skillet to the oven. Cook until the steaks reach the desired internal temperature.
  5. Remove the steaks from the oven and let them rest for 5–10 minutes.
  6. For the sauce, combine the red wine, balsamic vinegar, brown sugar, salt, and pepper in a small saucepan.
  7. Bring the sauce to a boil, then reduce to a simmer until it reduces by about half and thickens.
  8. Remove the sauce from the heat and stir in the butter. Spoon the sauce over the steaks before serving.

Planning Information

Equipment Needed

  • Oven
  • Stovetop
  • Oven-safe skillet
  • Small saucepan
  • Tongs
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Food thermometer
  • Oven mitts

Before Cooking

  • Let the steaks sit out for about 30 minutes before cooking.
  • Preheat the oven to 415°F.
  • Measure out the sauce ingredients before beginning.
  • Make sure the skillet is oven-safe.

If Dinner Is at 7:00 PM

I would start around 6:00 PM. This gives enough time for the steaks to sit out, the oven to preheat, the steak and sauce to cook, and the steak to rest before serving.

Serving 8 People

The original recipe serves 2 people. To serve 8 people, the recipe would need to be multiplied by four.

  • 8 filet mignon steaks
  • 4 tablespoons vegetable or canola oil
  • 4 sprigs rosemary
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 cups dry red wine
  • 2 cups balsamic vinegar
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 4 tablespoons brown sugar

Because eight steaks would not fit in one skillet, they would probably need to be cooked in batches or in multiple pans.

Dietary Information

This recipe contains beef, butter, and red wine. It is not vegetarian, vegan, dairy-free, or alcohol-free. Someone with specific dietary restrictions would need to check the exact ingredients being used.

Serving and Leftovers

The steak should be served warm after resting. Leftovers should be refrigerated in a covered container and used within about 3–4 days.
