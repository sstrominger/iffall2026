Behind the Icon

Marilyn Monroe was one of the most recognizable and influential figures of the 20th century. A gifted actress and model, she captivated audiences with her beauty, charm, and screen presence while also facing personal challenges away from the spotlight.

Beyond her glamorous Hollywood image, Monroe was an ambitious performer whose life reflected both the opportunities and pressures of fame.
