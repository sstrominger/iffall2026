Public Image

Marilyn Monroe became an enduring symbol of Hollywood glamour, while her public identity was shaped by photography, fashion, and the media.

Beauty & Style

Monroe's blonde curls, red lipstick, and glamorous wardrobe became recognizable elements of her public image.

Studio portraits and publicity photographs helped establish a carefully constructed image of femininity and celebrity.

Fame & the Media

Newspapers, magazines, and Hollywood publicity played important roles in shaping how audiences understood Monroe.

Her appearance and relationships often received as much attention as her artistic work. This tension between public fascination and personal identity became central to her story.
